The throwback party, right next door.

Edison and Woodbridge are neighbors on the Middlesex map. If you’re searching “90s party near me” from an Edison zip, the closest grown & sexy throwback party is ours — 10 minutes south on Route 9 at Lake Chateau Banquets, our flagship room.
