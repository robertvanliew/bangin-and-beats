What to Expect

Doors, drinks, three rounds, a dance floor.

  1. Doors open, bar opens, the DJ is already on the decks. Bring a valid ID — it’s strictly 30 & Up. Grab your custom 5x5 song card at the door: 24 titles from that night’s 60‑song pool, with BANGIN’ in the center square.
  2. The Hype Emcee takes the mic. Quick breakdown of the rules, a little crowd call‑and‑response, and round one drops while you’re still settling in.
  3. Three rounds. Prize loot every round. 90s Rap, 90s R&B, 2000s Hip‑Hop Bangers, 2000s Slow Jams. Dance battles, lip‑sync showdowns and sing‑alongs between rounds. Nobody sits still by round two.
  4. Then the floor opens up. The song cards go down but the DJ keeps going. About three hours from doors to last call — stay, dance, and meet the table you didn’t know before the first round.

FAQ

Common questions from the neighborhood.

  • Where exactly is Lake Chateau Banquets?

    1002 US-9, Woodbridge Township, NJ 07095. Right on Route 9 between the Turnpike exit and the mall. Free lot on site.

  • Is there food?

    Full menu at the venue, with sit-down service. You order at your table before the show. Your ticket doesn’t include food unless a specific event says so.

  • How do I get here from New York?

    Turnpike South to exit 11, then Route 9 south for a few miles. About 40 minutes from Manhattan off-peak. Rideshare works too.

  • Is the Woodbridge throwback party really 30 & Up?

    Yes. Every public Bangin’ & Beats night is strictly 30 & Up, no exceptions. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID; the door won’t admit anyone without one.

  • How often do you throw parties in Woodbridge?

    Woodbridge is our flagship room, and new tour dates are dropping soon. Check the tour dates page for the current calendar, and lock in your ticket early.

  • Do you throw parties in other NJ cities?

    Woodbridge is home base for public nights. Private parties run across NJ and NYC: we bring the host, the DJ, the custom song cards and the prize loot to your venue. Within 50 miles of NYC is our standard travel area.
