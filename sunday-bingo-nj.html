The Sunday Format

Why Sunday bingo hits different.

Saturday nights are crowded. Sunday afternoons are the move. Fewer people booked, better parking, brunch energy that stretches into the golden hour. When we run a Sunday day party, doors open at 3 PM and the show starts at 4 — you’re on the dance floor by round two and home by dinner. Or you stay for the after‑party that runs to 9 PM.

The format is the same as our Friday/Saturday nights: DJ drops 30‑second clips of hip‑hop and R&B throwbacks, you daub the squares on your card, five in a row wins the round. Cash prizes every round. Three rounds. Dance battles and lip sync wars between.

Sunday nights sell out even faster than weekends because the runtime works for parents, folks who work Monday mornings, and anyone who’s tired of Sunday dinner being the only plan.

What Sunday Looks Like

3PM to 9PM. All the daylight bingo you need.

  1. 3:00 PM — Doors open. You get your card at the door, take your table, order food from the venue kitchen. Full bar. Kick off your Sunday like you mean it.
  2. 4:00 PM — Show starts. Host warms up the room, DJ Tameil settles into the first set. Round one drops around 4:20.
  3. 4:20 – 7:00 PM — Three rounds of bingo. Each round runs ~30 minutes with a dance break and lip sync war between. Cash prizes at every round.
  4. 7:00 – 9:00 PM — After party. Card game closes but the DJ keeps going. Full dance floor. Some people leave for dinner. Some people stay for the whole thing.

Why Sunday Works

Who this night is actually for.

  • Parents who need to be home for the sitter — done by 9 PM, in bed by 10.
  • People who work Monday morning — a full night out that doesn’t ruin the week.
  • The brunch‑then‑something crew — start at brunch, roll into music bingo, home by dinner.
  • Long‑weekend visitors — Friday and Saturday are for the wedding / concert / event you flew in for. Sunday afternoon is when your friends actually want to hang.
  • People who don’t drink much — daytime energy, easier to nurse one drink across four hours.

FAQ

Sunday-specific questions.

  • Is there Sunday bingo THIS weekend?

    Check /events for the current calendar — public Sunday nights drop every 6–8 weeks. If nothing’s on the calendar, get on the newsletter to hear when the next one goes on sale.

  • What time is a Sunday night?

    Doors 3 PM, show 4 PM, three rounds until ~7 PM, after‑party until 9 PM. Home well before dinner if you want.

  • Are Sunday nights 21+ too?

    Yes. Public events at Lake Chateau are 21+ per the venue license.

  • Can I book a private Sunday?

    Absolutely — Sunday day parties are our most-requested private booking format. Perfect for milestone birthdays, bachelorettes, family reunions, or corporate teams that don’t want to spend a Friday night on work stuff.

  • Is there food?

    Full kitchen at the venue. Order at your table. Ticket doesn’t include food unless the specific event says so.

  • How many people fit?

    ~125 seated at Lake Chateau. Every night sells out. Grab your ticket when the calendar drops.
