The Music Pool

The Active Music Pool: 90s & 00s Hip‑Hop & R&B Anthems.

Every Bangin’ & Beats™ night runs on a curated 60‑song pool of 90s and 2000s hip‑hop and R&B. The DJ spins 30‑to‑60 second clips; if the title is on your song card, you lock it in. Line up five in a row and shout “THAT’S BANGIN’!” to claim the round.

Below: the four era rounds we pull from: a 100‑song catalog (each night draws a 60‑song pool), anthems from Boyz II Men and Biggie all the way to Beyoncé and 50 Cent. Test your ears on THE BEAT BOARD™, free at banginandbeats.com/play.
