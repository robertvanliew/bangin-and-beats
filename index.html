How it works

How we rock the room.

Forget everything you know about old-school nights out. Our hosts command the floor, the DJ drops the heavy bass cuts, and the crowd is the main attraction. The rules are simple: track the hits, lock down a row, and claim the night.

  1. Grab your tracklist card

    When you walk through the doors, we hand you a heavy, custom 5x5 Song Card packed with 24 legendary throwback titles—classic 90s R&B jams, early 2000s hip-hop club bangers, and undisputed street anthems. No two cards are identical, so you’re chasing your own path to a win. The center spot holds our brand-new “BANGIN’” logo space.

  2. The DJ drops a jam

    Our DJ turns up the pressure, dropping a 30-to-60 second live clip of an absolute throwback classic. Widescreen video monitors and low neon lights pulse with the vibe. No numbers called here—just pure heat.

  3. Match the vibe, sing the hook

    Recognize the track? Mark your square on THE BEAT BOARD™ immediately! Group singing isn’t just allowed—it’s mandatory. Sing the hook at the top of your lungs with the rest of the crowd. Hear the track. Lock it in. Claim the night.™

  4. Win big, stay for the dance battle

    Be the first to line up 5 matching squares in a row, column, or diagonal? Stand up and command the floor by shouting “THAT’S BANGIN’!” Our Hype Emcee verifies your card on the spot, the crowd erupts, and you walk away with premium prize loot.

Between the rounds

What happens between rounds?

Cards down, lights up. Hosts pull people out of their seats for a quick battle and the winner walks away with another prize. Sit it out and you get a front‑row seat to whoever’s brave enough.

  • Dance Battles

    Two players, one beat, thirty seconds each. The room votes with applause and the host has the tiebreak. We close every night on a full dance battle, but smaller ones pop up between rounds too.

  • Lip Sync Wars

    One song, one hook, no points for shyness. We hand over the mic and you give us the music video. Best performance takes a bar tab or something off the prize shelf.

  • Sing-Alongs

    The whole room becomes the chorus. Loudest table on the hook wins a round of shots or an extra prize draw. If you came with your crew, this is where they earn their keep.

What is Bangin’ & Beats™?

BANGIN’ & BEATS™ is a premium, live interactive throwback party hosted across major New York and New Jersey nightlife venues, operating proudly under the High Class Experience LLC corporate shield.

We completely blend a fun, crowd-powered music game with a high-energy throwback dance party. Our mature, style-conscious 30 & Up demographic enjoys private VIP lounge booths, custom sparkler bottle service presentations, and elite prize giveaways.

What is an interactive hip-hop throwback party?

An interactive throwback party is a live night out where the crowd plays along with the music instead of just listening. At Bangin’ & Beats, every guest gets a custom 5×5 song card with 24 titles drawn from a 60-song pool of 90s and 2000s hip-hop and R&B, plus the BANGIN’ center square. The DJ spins 30-to-60 second clips; if the title is on your card, you mark it. The first guest to line up five in a row, column, or diagonal shouts “THAT’S BANGIN’!” to win the round. A typical night runs three rounds across about three hours, with dance battles, lip-sync showdowns, and group sing-alongs between rounds and a mainstage dance battle to close.

How does the interactive party night work, step by step?
  1. Grab your tracklist card. Every guest gets a unique 5×5 song card with 24 titles from that night’s 60‑song pool, plus the BANGIN’ center square.
  2. The DJ drops a jam. A 30-to-60 second clip of a throwback classic plays. If the title is on your card, you mark the square.
  3. Match the vibe, sing the hook. Tracks drop roughly every 30–60 seconds. Singing along isn’t just allowed, it’s mandatory.
  4. Line up five and shout. First guest to line up five squares (row, column, or diagonal) shouts “THAT’S BANGIN’!” and the Hype Emcee verifies the card.
  5. Three rounds a night. Premium prizes every round. Between rounds: dance battles, lip-sync showdowns, and sing-alongs. The night closes on a mainstage dance battle.

FAQ · Liner Notes

The Bangin’ & Beats™ FAQ. Liner notes for the curious.

Everything you wanted to ask before grabbing a ticket to a BANGIN’ & BEATS™ night. Still curious? Send us a note.

  1. How does the interactive party night actually work?

    Our DJ spins a 30-to-60 second live clip of a throwback anthem, and you track the title on your custom 5x5 song card. Be the first to line up five in a row, column, or diagonal, then stand up and shout “THAT’S BANGIN’!” Our Hype Emcee verifies your card on the spot and you walk away with prize loot. We run three rounds a night, with dance battles and sing-alongs in between.

  2. Do I need to recognize every single song to win prizes?

    No. The song title appears on the screens behind the host, so you just match it to the squares on your card. Knowing the words is a bonus for singing along, not a requirement for winning.

  3. How long does a Bangin’ & Beats™ event last?

    About three hours from doors to last call. We host on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, and start times vary by venue, so check the tour dates page for your night. Each night is three interactive music rounds plus a mainstage dance battle to close.

  4. How much are tickets and what do they include?

    General admission is $35. Your ticket gets you in the door, a custom 5x5 song card for every round, a shot at the prizes, and about three hours of live DJ, host, and dance-battle energy. VIP tables and bottle service are reserved separately through our booking team.

  5. Is it really strictly 30 & Up? Do I need an ID to enter?

    Yes. Every public Bangin’ & Beats night is strictly 30 & Up, no exceptions. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID: driver’s license, passport, or state ID. The door checks IDs and won’t admit anyone without one.

  6. Can I reserve a VIP table for my crew or a birthday party?

    Yes. VIP tables and private lounge booths, with bottle service, are reserved through our booking form or by texting 201-344-4589 with your date and group size. Otherwise, general admission seating is first-come, first-served.

  7. Where do you host your events?

    At premier nightclubs and upscale lounges across New York and New Jersey, with our flagship room in Woodbridge, NJ. For private parties we pull up to your venue, office, or house. Within 50 miles of NYC is our standard travel area.

  8. Is Bangin’ & Beats™ based in NYC or New Jersey?

    Both. We’re based in NY/NJ, and public tour dates run at venues across both states. Bangin’ & Beats is produced by High Class Experience LLC.

  9. How far do you travel for private parties and celebrations?

    Within 50 miles of NYC is our standard travel area. Further out, we add simple travel fees based on distance. Tell us your city and we’ll come back with a quote. A 50% deposit holds your date.

  10. Where can I find your upcoming multi-city tour dates?

    Every public date, venue, and ticket link lives on our tour dates page. Join the email list at the bottom of this page to hear about new cities and early ticket drops first.

  11. Can you bring this interactive experience to my city or company?

    Yes. We bring the show to corporate events, brand activations, galas, and private celebrations. If there’s a room and a crowd, we build a custom night with your playlist, song cards, and prizes. Use the booking form to start the conversation.

  12. What happens if I show up late to the party?

    Start times vary by venue, so check your night on the tour dates page. Aim to arrive about 15 minutes before the first round to grab a drink and your song card. If you arrive mid-round, you jump right in; we don’t pause the music for late arrivals.

  13. Can I get a refund if my plans change?

    All ticket sales are final: no refunds and no transfers. If we cancel a night for any reason, you’ll get a full refund automatically. Questions? info@banginandbeats.com.

  14. What’s the dress code? What do people wear?

    Grown and sexy with a throwback twist: think throwback jerseys, denim on denim, velour tracksuits, gold chains, and fresh sneakers, or dress up for the lounge. There’s no strict dress code, but the photos hit harder when the room looks like a time machine.
