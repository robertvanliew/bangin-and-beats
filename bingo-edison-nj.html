The Local Night

Music bingo, right next door.

Edison and Woodbridge are neighbors on the Middlesex map. If you’re searching “bingo near me” from an Edison zip, the closest recurring music bingo night is ours — 10 minutes south on Route 9 at Lake Chateau Banquets.

The nights are 21+. Format is: DJ Tameil drops 30‑second clips of throwback hip‑hop and R&B (80s through 2000s), you daub the songs on your 5×5 card, five in a row wins the round. Cash prizes every round. Three rounds per night. Dance battles and lip sync wars between.

Every public night has sold out. Buy tickets early.
