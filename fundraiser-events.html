Why This Beats a Bake Sale

Fundraisers are boring. This one isn’t.

The average school fundraiser raises $3–5 per attendee. A throwback party night raises $25–50 per attendee, because people pay for a ticket AND stay for the drinks/food/raffle upsells. Everyone plays. Everyone dances. Nobody feels like they gave “out of obligation.”

We’ve run fundraisers for PTAs (elementary through high school), church groups, youth sports teams, veterans’ nonprofits, and community centers. The format works whether your crowd is 50 people or 300+.

We handle the entertainment side end‑to‑end. Your team handles ticketing, venue, and food. We’ll walk you through the ticket pricing math on the intro call so the fundraiser actually clears money.

The Fundraiser Format

How a fundraiser night actually runs.

  1. Discovery call — free, 20 minutes. We ask about the org, the cause, the audience age range, the venue you have in mind, and what you’re trying to raise. From that we recommend a ticket price, throwback playlist theme, and expected attendance target.
  2. Custom throwback playlist built to the crowd. PTA fundraiser for parents of school‑age kids? The 90s R&B Vibe Set plus 2000s Hip‑Hop Bangers. Church group? Same energy, cleaner edits, no harder tracks. Alumni night? The era they graduated in. We build the playlist for the people who’ll actually be in the room.
  3. We show up and run the entertainment. Hype Emcee on the mic, DJ with full pro sound, custom 5×5 song cards, markers, prize logistics. The DJ spins 30–60 second clips, and the first guest to five in a row shouts “THAT’S BANGIN’!” Setup is ~45 minutes. Fundraisers typically start with an intro from the org leader, then hand it to us.
  4. Raffle + auction integration. Between rounds is prime raffle‑drawing time. Our Hype Emcee will hand the mic to your org rep to draw raffles, take bids on silent auction items, or run the “heads or tails” upsell. All included in the format.
  5. Post-event: photos + wrap. We hand you the group photos and the total attendance count. You handle the thank‑you email.

Ticket Math

A rough model for what you’ll clear.

Take a typical PTA fundraiser as an example:

  • Venue: school gym or auditorium — usually free for the org
  • Guests: 150 parents & community
  • Ticket price: $35 pp (typical fundraiser floor)
  • Gross ticket revenue: $5,250
  • Our fee: $2,000 (turn‑key including all setup)
  • Raffle upsell: $10 per raffle ticket, ~$800 total
  • Silent auction: variable, typically $500–2,000
  • Net to the org: $4,500–6,000

Actual numbers depend on your ticket price, venue costs, and how aggressive the raffle/auction is. We walk through the math on the intro call.

FAQ

Common questions from fundraiser organizers.

  • Can a fundraiser throwback party be all-ages?

    Yes. Our public tour shows are strictly 30 & Up, but private fundraisers can be all-ages depending on your venue. The playlist gets adjusted to family‑friendly (we skip the harder tracks). We’ve done school family nights and youth ministry events.

  • Do you offer nonprofit pricing?

    Yes. Registered 501(c)(3)s get a ~15% discount off our standard private booking rate. Repeat annual bookings get an additional discount.

  • Do you handle ticket sales?

    Not directly — your org runs ticket sales through Eventbrite, Zeffy, or however you already do fundraising. We can recommend platforms and walk through pricing.

  • How far do you travel?

    We are based in NY/NJ. Within 50 miles of NYC is our standard travel area; further out, we just tack on simple travel lines. A 50% deposit holds your calendar date.

  • How long does a fundraiser night run?

    Usually 3–3.5 hours total. Doors, three interactive music rounds, raffle draws, and one closing dance/mingle set.

  • Can we brand the song cards with our org?

    Yes. Optional add‑on. Cards can include your org name, logo, and fundraiser theme.
