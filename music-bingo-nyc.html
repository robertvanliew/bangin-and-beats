The NYC Format

Music bingo that fits the room you already have.

NYC doesn’t have a recurring hip‑hop and R&B music bingo night. There are music bingo brands doing 80s rock, country, and Top 40. Nobody’s doing what we do — 80s / 90s / 2000s hip‑hop and R&B, with a comedian‑style host and a real DJ rig. So the way to get it in NYC is to book us for a private event.

We travel to the city constantly. Manhattan Financial District office happy hours, Brooklyn rooftop bachelorette parties, Queens family birthdays, Bronx block parties. If you have a venue and 15+ people, we bring the game.

Public nights are at Lake Chateau Banquets in Woodbridge, NJ — 45 minutes from Midtown via Turnpike. Straight shot for anyone who wants to see the format before booking.

NYC Venues That Work

Where we’ve set up before.

  • Rooftop bars — Brooklyn, LES, Midtown. Sound levels usually work; check with the venue about DJ setup.
  • Office event spaces — Midtown / FiDi corporate happy hours are our most-booked NYC format. Client sends the guest list, we handle everything else.
  • Restaurant private rooms — back rooms in Chinatown, LES, Williamsburg. We coordinate music levels with the venue in advance.
  • Warehouse spaces — Greenpoint, Bushwick, LIC studio spaces for 100+ person events.
  • Hotel event rooms — if the wedding’s at a Manhattan hotel, we run the bachelorette in the same building the night before.
  • Home / townhouse — Park Slope brownstones, UWS apartments with roof access. 15–30 people range.

If you’re unsure whether your venue works, send us the details and we’ll tell you what’s needed.

FAQ

NYC-specific questions.

  • Do you run public nights in NYC?

    Not currently — public nights are at Lake Chateau Banquets in Woodbridge, NJ (45 min from Midtown). NYC is private bookings only for now. If interest grows we’ll add a public NYC residency.

  • Which boroughs do you serve?

    All five. Manhattan and Brooklyn are highest volume. Queens, Bronx, and Staten Island bookings all get quoted — extra time for load‑in factors in.

  • Do you handle noise ordinances / venue coordination?

    Yes. We coordinate with the venue on sound levels and any city noise ordinance rules before the event. That’s built into our setup checklist.

  • Can you do all-Beyoncé / all-Jay-Z / era-specific NYC nights?

    Yes. That’s the point of custom song pools. Tell us the theme and we build a 60-song pool around it.

  • Load-in / setup time?

    30 minutes typically. Rooftops and walk‑up buildings add 15–30 min. We plan around your event start time.

  • Do you provide liquor / catering?

    No — that’s your venue’s job. We bring the game only.
