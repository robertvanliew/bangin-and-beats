The Newark-Adjacent Night

Newark has clubs. Newark has bars. Nobody threw a grown & sexy throwback party like this — until now.

If you’re in Newark searching for a “90s party near me”, you already know the truth — the city’s nightlife skews young. What Newark doesn’t have is a DJ‑led, strictly 30 & Up, interactive hip‑hop and R&B throwback party built for the people who lived the music the first time.

Bangin’ & Beats throws exactly that at Lake Chateau Banquets in Woodbridge Township, our flagship room — roughly 20 minutes down the Turnpike. It’s a straight shot from Downtown Newark, closer than most people realize.

Every public night is strictly 30 & Up with valid ID, about three hours from doors to last call. The DJ spins 30‑to‑60 second clips of 90s & 00s hip‑hop and R&B — 90s Rap, 90s R&B, 2000s Hip‑Hop Bangers, 2000s Slow Jams — and if the title’s on your song card, you lock it in. First to five in a row shouts “THAT’S BANGIN’!” Premium prize loot every round. Dance battles between.

Get Directions

From Downtown Newark: NJ Turnpike South to exit 11, then Route 9 south for a few miles. Off‑peak: 20 minutes. Rush hour: 30–35.

From Ironbound / East Ward: Same route, add ~5 min for local streets to Turnpike entrance.

From Weequahic / South Ward: Route 22 to the Parkway south, or straight down Route 27. About 25 minutes.

Public transit? Not easy — the venue is on a highway. Rideshare from Newark runs about $30–40 one way. If you’re rolling with a crew of 4+, that splits down fine.

Parking: Free on‑site lot at Lake Chateau. Get there before doors if you can.

FAQ

Common questions from the Newark side.

  • Do you throw parties actually IN Newark?

    Public nights run at our flagship room in Woodbridge, about 20 minutes south. Private parties run everywhere in Essex County, including Newark itself: restaurants, lounges, event spaces, and private homes.

  • Is the throwback party worth the drive from Newark?

    If you came up on 90s and 2000s hip-hop and R&B, yes. It’s a live DJ, a Hype Emcee, three interactive music rounds and a strictly 30 & Up room, and it’s a straight shot down the Turnpike.

  • Can I bring a crew from Newark?

    Yes. VIP tables and private lounge booths are reserved through our booking form or by texting 201-344-4589 with your date and group size. Otherwise, general admission seating is first-come, first-served.

  • What can I win?

    Premium prize loot every round, three rounds a night. First to five in a row, column, or diagonal shouts “THAT’S BANGIN’!” and the Hype Emcee verifies your card on the spot.

  • How often do you throw public nights?

    New tour dates are dropping soon. Every public date and ticket link lives on the tour dates page, and tickets are $35.
